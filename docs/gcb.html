This is a floor, not a full count. Sales with no caveat lodged (many GCBs are bought through trusts or companies) never reach URA's data. URA gives one location per street, so a few sales on streets that sit on a GCB area's edge are matched by distance (≤150 m, ≥1,400 m² land) and tagged “edge”. Some URA records have no location at all; those streets are placed using OneMap addresses.
