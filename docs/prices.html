Why are some towns more expensive?

"Expected" = what the town's distance to Raffles Place and typical flat age predict. The gap is everything else: views, estate, flat size, scarcity, schools, amenities. Big flats cost less per sq ft, so towns with large flats look cheaper on $psf.
