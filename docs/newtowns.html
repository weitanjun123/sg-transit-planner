Punggol & Sengkang vs Tengah

home · home prices · gap map

Do new towns' prices follow the same path? Punggol and Sengkang went from empty land to mature towns over 20 years; Tengah, the "forest town", got its first residents in August 2023. Here are their starting prices (BTO), their resale paths, and what early BTO buyers ended up with — so Tengah can be tracked against them as its own resales begin.

1 · Starting price: what BTO buyers paid

BTO launch prices for Sengkang, Punggol and Tengah (midpoint of HDB's price range, before grants), in dollars or as a share of the average HDB resale $psf that year (below 100% = cheaper than an average resale flat, which lets a 2008 launch and a 2023 launch be compared fairly).

No BTO price data before FY2008: HDB's open data starts in April 2008, so Punggol's and Sengkang's early BTO prices (late 1990s to 2007) can't be charted. Their lines start at FY2008.

2 · The resale path, lined up by town age

Each town's median resale $psf compared with the whole HDB market, by years since the town's first resales (year 0: Sengkang 2002, Punggol 2005; Tengah expected around 2028 when its first flats reach MOP). Sengkang's handful of 1980s farm-area flats, sold in the 1990s before the new town existed, are left out. Above 100% = dearer than the average HDB flat.

3 · What early BTO buyers got at MOP

For each launch year: BTO price vs the median resale price of the same flat type, same town, built that year, sold in the first two years after its 5-year MOP. "Beyond market" strips out how much the whole HDB market rose over the same years.

3b · Myth check: does a 5-room make more money than a 3-room?

Every BTO launch in HDB's price data (14 non-mature towns, FY2008 onwards) whose flats have reached MOP: BTO price vs the median resale price of the same flat type, same town, built that year, sold in the first two years after MOP. Profit shown both ways — in dollars and as % of what you paid.
