SG Transit Planner

An open-source tool that uses public data to find where Singaporeans live more than a 10-minute walk from any MRT station, today and after every line LTA has announced, and suggests where new stations and lines could go. It gives more weight to homes whose nearest station is already packed at rush hour.

Gap map →Underserved homes, new BTO and land-sale sites, LTA's planned network by year, and proposed lines with the reasons for each. Ridership compare →Month on month, year on year or any 2 months, for one station, your stations, a line or the whole network. Early wakers & night owls →Which stations wake earliest and sleep latest, whether distance from the CBD explains it, and the busiest point-to-point trips. Deep dive →Hidden line changes, who buses to the MRT, minutes on the train by town, station personalities, weekends, and trips that don't come back by MRT. 24-hour heartbeat →Every station-to-station trip pulsing through one weekday. Home prices & the MRT →HDB resale (and condo, once the URA key is in) $psf by distance to MRT, what happened when new stations opened, and prices by town. Does it make financial sense? →Benefit vs cost of every proposed line and station, more trains, infill vs express bus, running fewer empty trains, and late-night trains. Pixel MRT sim →Watch rush hour build up train by train. Tap a train to look inside, and ride Jurong East → Raffles Place.

Data: LTA DataMall (passenger volume, platform crowd), URA Master Plan 2019 rail layer, HDB Property Information via data.gov.sg, HDB building footprints, LTA/URA/HDB announcements (sources are listed in the data files). Built to support planners, not to replace them. Proposals are screening-level, not engineering alignments. Source code (MIT)
